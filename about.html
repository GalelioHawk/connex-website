About CONNEX

CONNEX is the brand of CONNEX DIGITAL, a South African technology company building digital platforms designed to make useful services more accessible.

Connex Edu

Connex Edu is an education technology platform operated by CONNEX DIGITAL. It brings learning resources and tutoring tools together in one place, helping learners access study materials while enabling tutors and learners to manage tutoring interactions digitally.

  • DBE past-paper resources and study notes for Grades 10–12, with availability depending on the catalogue and verified source links.
  • A learner help board for posting study questions.
  • Tutor discovery and connection requests.
  • Lesson bookings and links to external online meetings.
  • Tutor student rosters, homework assignments and learner submissions.
  • Homework marks and assessment feedback.
  • A manual tutoring payment ledger for recording charges, payments and balances.

These tools are available through the account-based platform. Learning resources and the help board are free to access; tutors may charge for lessons. The payment ledger tracks records and does not process online payments.
