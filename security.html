Security

Connex Edu is operated by CONNEX DIGITAL, a South African private company (CIPC registration K2026189508).

Account security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt on the server. Account sessions have a limited validity period. Protected application functions validate account sessions on the server, and sign-in requests have server-side rate limiting.

Use a unique password, keep your credentials private and log out on shared devices. Contact us if you suspect unauthorised account access.

Learning and tutoring information

Account-based learning and tutoring tools require sign-in. Learner records, homework, bookings and payment-ledger details are not published on the public company-information pages or listed in the website sitemap.

See our Privacy Policy for how personal information is handled and how to make a privacy request.

Report a security issue

Email founder@connexsa.co.za with the affected page or feature, steps to reproduce the issue and its potential impact. Please avoid sending passwords, session tokens or another person's private data.

Give us a reasonable opportunity to investigate before publicly disclosing a vulnerability. Do not access, change or extract other users' information when reporting an issue.

No service can guarantee absolute security. If you notice an issue, please contact us.
